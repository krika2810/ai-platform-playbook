Season 2 AI platform solution architect. Episode 3: Multi-tenant service. If you are working as AI platform solution architect in any of the enterprises, you might have seen multiple LOBs building AI projects. And for all these LOBs, you must have a platform. You cannot have single platform for each LOB. That'll be a cost overhead. In that case, you must have a centralized AI platform for all these LOBs to access. So how do you design a sophisticated, successful AI platform for all your LOBs? First thing is you must create hard tenant isolation in your AI platform so that each LOB will access one particular area in the AI platform. And second thing is you must have usage metering for each LOBs so that you can measure the usage details. And third thing is you must have the custom rate limiting for each LOB to access this platform. And finally, you must prioritize the usage through the model routing gateway so that you can provide right service to the right LOB. Follow all these design principles and be an successful AI platform solution architect in your career. All the best.
