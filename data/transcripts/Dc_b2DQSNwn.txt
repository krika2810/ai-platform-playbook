Season two, AI platform solution architect transformation, episode seven: enterprise evaluation platform. When you build Gen AI or AI agent project, one of the critical tasks is to evaluate the output produced by the large language model. How do you evaluate the output produced by large language model effectively? You must need to build automated pipeline using LLM-as-a-judge architecture. And also, you must need to use the curated data in the data set, and produce the ideal output, and use the semantic regression test in the continuous integration pipeline to test it out. This way, you can evaluate the LLM properly, and give it to the team who are building this LLM agent or AI agent project effectively for your organization. So follow this best practice as an AI platform solution architect, and help your organization out. All the best.
