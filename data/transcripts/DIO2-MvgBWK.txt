Three interesting facts about IBM's Bee AI agentic framework. First, it is inspired by the nature. If you look at honeycomb where honeybees live in colonies and each honey acts on one particular task to achieve the global task, so it is inspired by the nature as each individual honeybees working as AI agent. In the Bee AI agentic framework, there are multiple small agents will work on particular task to achieve a global task. Second, it uses swarm intelligence. What exactly swarm intelligence? If you take ant who are living in colonies or bird flocks where birds are living in group, each bird or each ant has a particular task, and it has its particular rule to achieve the task. At the same time, all these ants and birds work together and achieve a global task for their colony. So, it uses swarm intelligence to achieve global task. And third, it's energy efficient. IBM's Bee AI agent is energy efficient. What exactly it means, it can be deployed in your on-premises environment with your less resource capacity.
