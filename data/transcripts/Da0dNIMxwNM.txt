When you appear for AI architect interview, sometimes they may ask you to white board key items. These are all the main key areas that you must need to focus and be ready to whiteboard with the interviewer. And first one is AI agent orchestration flow. How AI agent orchestration works, how multiple agents communicate with each other, and how you are going to achieve their use case with a multi-agent workflow. And second thing is state management and memory, tool selection. How the agents are maintaining the states, and how agents are selecting the tool to perform a task. So you must need to know how to whiteboard these details for the interviewer. And third one, you must need to know how to explain the security and governance part of AI agent orchestration. Master all these three areas and get pass through the interview. All the best.
