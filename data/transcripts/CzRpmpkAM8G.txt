Here are the some of the key benefits of becoming an enterprise architect. If you are performing a role of solution architect or cloud architect, you must be proceeding in the path of enterprise architect, if you are interested. So, what are all the benefits of getting the role to the enterprise architect? First one, so far, you have been focusing on one particular area, for example, infrastructure solutioning, or platform solutioning, or application solutioning, and so on. But when you become enterprise architect, your view will expand, starting from the business architecture, data architecture, technology architecture, application architecture, and implementation governance, operational excellence, all those areas. So, in order to get this role, you must have the opportunity to work such a project in your organization, and also, if you are capable enough, and if you have some time, take a look on TOGAF certification.
