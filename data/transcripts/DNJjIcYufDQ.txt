This is one of the architectures used to build the model, and version five is the current version. And OSS is open source software. And 120 and 20 are the billion parameters used to train these models. And GPT-OSS 120 is used for the enterprise applications. This needs a lot of resources. So if you are an enterprise, go and explore this model. And GPT-OSS 20 billion parameters can be used in your desktop or laptop for your personal work or your small-scale application works.
