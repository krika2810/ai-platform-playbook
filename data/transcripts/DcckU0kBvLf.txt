Season two AI platform solution architect transformation. Episode one basics of platform. Before getting into AI platform solution architect roles and responsibilities and what are all the key tasks that AI platform solution architect does in an AI industry, you must need to understand what exactly the platform means. Platform is the layer between infrastructure and application. Imagine that you have a big cake piece. In that cake piece, you may have multiple layers. The bottom most layer is called infrastructure, and the middle layer where you have the cream, chocolate cookies, those are all called the platform layer. On top of that, you may have the name, beautiful cream and decoration, that is called your application. So, platform is sitting between infrastructure and the application. So, the platform is very critical for the applications to run fine in any infrastructure. The person who is managing this particular platform may be called platform engineer or platform solution architect. So, this platform can be provisioned in any of the infrastructure type, whether on-premises or in the cloud. In the cloud also it is called cloud infrastructure, cloud platform, and cloud applications. In the on-premises, it's called plain infrastructure, platform, and application. So, now we got to know about what exactly the platform means, right? And let's get into the first episode of what exactly the platform architect does in the AI world. So, in classic world, the platform architect takes care of building the multiple tools which are required for the applications team to run fine their application on the platform. And they take care of the platform layer that includes, for example, OpenShift is one of the platforms for the applications to run fine. In AI world, the platform architect or AI platform architect must take the responsibility of setting up the clusters, nodes which can be utilized by the application teams. For example, if the application team wants to run AI agent application on a platform, the platform architect must know the basics of how to provision this GPU and on top of that how you can deploy the LLMs and how they can support the application team to run fine their AI agent application. So, in your organization, there might be multiple LOBs. So, each LOB may be running or creating one agent application, AI agent application. So, if you have like 10 to 15 LOBs, 10 to 15 different application development team builds the AI agent application or multi-agent application. So, as a AI platform solution architect, you must take care of building the common tools that can be utilized by all these LOBs, so that the duplicates can be avoided. So, that is called internal developer platform. So, as a AI platform architect, you must take the responsibility of building AI internal developer platform for your organization to deploy and run their applications fine.
