Cloud reliability is the ability of your workload consistently available for your customer. If any failure occurs to your workload, it must automatically recover from the failure. And you must know the recovery procedures. And if there is a need for your workload to scale horizontally, it must be horizontally scalable to increase the availability. And you must not guess the capacity. Always have the monitoring tool or system to measure the capacity of your workload and provision that capacity. And any platform or infrastructure failure occurs, your workload must be consistently available for your customer all the time.
