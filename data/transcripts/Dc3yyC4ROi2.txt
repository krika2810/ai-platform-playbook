Season 2 AI Platform Solutions Architect Transformation, episode six RAG as a service. If you are working as an AI platform solution architect in your organization you might have seen multiple development teams might have developed the AI project with different AI architectures like RAG architecture, REACT architecture, human in the loop architecture etc. So, when each individual team develops these type of AI project, they must need to introduce the document pipeline, they must need to introduce the vector indexing, and they must need to introduce the hybrid search like dense and sparse. So, these are all the key components of AI project implementation. So, when these teams create this many searches and this many vector indexing and this many document pipelines, your platform will be messy. So, in that case, how you will address this problem as an AI platform solutions architect? You must provision this RAG architecture as a service so that the teams will not build these redundant mechanisms to access it. So, you must need to provide RAG architecture as a service that consists of document pipeline, then vector indexing, hybrid search like dense and sparse, all these components can be accessed as an API call. So that you can avoid the development teams building the redundant services. Think about provisioning RAG as a service, or REACT as a service, for your AI development teams as an AI platform solutions architect. All the best.
