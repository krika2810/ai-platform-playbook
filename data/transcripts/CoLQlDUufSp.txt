Follow these four pre-interview preparation tips. First, go through the company profile and understand what modernizations journey or cloud migration journey the company is in, and understand their strategy. Second, go through the job description and understand thoroughly, and match the job description with your experience, and make sure that your experience matches with job description perfectly. Third, pick your close friend and do mock interview. Once the mock interview is done, get the feedback from your friend, and if there are any mistakes, correct those. Fourth, get one key project or innovative project that you implemented in your previous company, and make sure that you have thorough knowledge on that particular project, and explain clearly to the interviewer.
