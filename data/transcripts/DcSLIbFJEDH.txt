Season 1, AI Solution Architect transformation, episode 10, resilient disaster recovery environment. When you work as AI infrastructure architect, you must definitely need to consider the disaster recovery environment while setting up your enterprise-grade AI production environment. So what exactly it means? Your vector databases must be in multiple regions. When one region fails, it must need to fail over to another region to serve your request. Also, your LLM must have the fall back gateway so that when the request comes to the first LLM and if it fails, it will fall back to the secondary LLM, which is running in different region. This way, your AI infrastructure will be resilient and it can withstand all the request in case of one failure occurs in one region, and it'll be handled by the another region.
