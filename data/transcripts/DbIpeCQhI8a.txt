What are the three AI architect roles that pay you 200K? One, Enterprise AI Architect. He who designs the enterprise AI strategies and focusing on the AI governance, and he aligns the AI with the business. Two, the Solution AI Architect or AI Solution Architect. He who designs the AI system end-to-end, and provides the solution to all AI-related problems. Three, the Cloud AI Architect. He who knows the AI services which are running in the cloud, and he builds the AI application which are cloud-native, and deploys the application in the cloud platform.
