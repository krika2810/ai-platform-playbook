If you want to be a successful solution architect or customer architect, follow this formula: 4I - E is equal to success. First I, investigate. Investigate customer's problem, pain points, modernization journey, digital transformation, etc. Second one, innovate. Provide innovative idea using IoT, AI, mobile technologies. Third one, initiate. Initiate the next step, initiate the next conversation, initiate the next action item for the customer to deploy the product. Fourth one, implement. Implement the solution access friendly, perfectly for the customer. And E is ego. Eliminate this ego while working with your peers and with the customers. This will lead you to great success. 4I - E is equal to success.
