Do you know that we are living in cloud 3.0? What exactly cloud 3.0 era? Prior to this cloud 3.0, we were living in cloud 2.0 where we utilized the cloud environment or infrastructure for deploying our workload, running our workload in the public cloud or private cloud. And now after A has come, we are living in cloud 3.0 era. What exactly it means, you can run your AI workloads in the cloud. At the same time, you can connect your AI workloads which are running in the cloud to the on-premises workload in your local environment by using hybrid cloud concept. And also you can utilize the AI capabilities provided by cloud service providers and also scale your AI system effortlessly. So, this era of integrating AI workloads with your cloud of choice is called Cloud 3.0.
