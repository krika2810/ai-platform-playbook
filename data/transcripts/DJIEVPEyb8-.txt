What is LangGraph? LangGraph is a framework that helps you to build AI agent, a powerful AI agent for your organization. And it is a framework on top of which you will use the large language models, tools, memory, and storage so that you can build a powerful AI agent. And that AI agent will not only respond to your queries, or respond to your task, or execute the task, but also it will go by its own. It will stop when some task fails, and if needs to be looped for executing multiple tasks. So these are all achieved with the help of LangGraph. So basically, if you imagine that LangChain is an engine of your car, and LangGraph is the GPS and control system that will make your car go in right direction and stop or start whenever needed.
