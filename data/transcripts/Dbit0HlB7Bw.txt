If you're an experienced developer or experienced architect and in the process of preparing a resume, keep following all these points. Keep the resume size as only two pages length, not more than that. And in that two pages, you must cover the profile summary, skills summary, key accomplishments that shows the value that you added to your organization. And next one is project experience. Do not have all the projects in your resume. Just collect select the key projects which are relevant to the job description you are going to apply, and the last section as the education. That's it.
